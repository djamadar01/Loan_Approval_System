BUSINESS RULES SUMMARY
================================================================================

DTI RISK LEVELS:
≤ 20%     → LOW      (Excellent)
20-36%    → LOW      (Good)
36-43%    → MEDIUM   (Acceptable)
43-50%    → HIGH     (High)
> 50%     → CRITICAL (Critical)

CREDIT SCORE LEVELS:
800+      → EXCELLENT (1.0% default)
750-799   → GOOD      (2.0% default)
670-749   → FAIR      (5.0% default)
580-669   → POOR      (15.0% default)
<580      → VERY_POOR (30.0% default)

LOAN-TO-INCOME RATIOS:
≤ 2.0x    → LOW      (1.0% default)
2.0-2.5x  → MEDIUM   (3.0% default)
2.5-3.0x  → HIGH     (7.0% default)
> 3.0x    → CRITICAL (15.0% default)

OVERALL RISK ALGORITHM:
Score ≥ 3.5 → CRITICAL (DENY)
Score ≥ 2.5 → HIGH (CONDITIONAL)
Score ≥ 1.5 → MEDIUM (REVIEW)
Score < 1.5 → LOW (APPROVE)
